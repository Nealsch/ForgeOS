# Decision Log

## Purpose

Document decisions, their context, and the status of follow-up actions so teams can stay aligned over time.

## When Used

Use this template whenever a decision is made that should be recorded for traceability and future reference.

## Produced By

Forge-Knowledge-Management or equivalent decision capture activity.

## Consumed By

* Architects
* Engineers
* Product owners
* QA
* Reviewers

## Template

### Decision ID

### Date

### Decision Summary

### Context

Describe the situation, goals, and constraints leading to the decision.

### Decision

Document the chosen approach or outcome.

### Alternatives Considered

List the viable alternatives and why they were rejected.

### Rationale

Capture the reasoning behind the selected decision.

### Consequences and Impact

Describe the expected effects and any trade-offs.

### Follow-Up Actions

List actions required to implement or monitor the decision.

### Status

Record whether the decision is Draft, Agreed, Implemented, or Deprecated.

## Completion Criteria

* Decision context and summary are documented.
* Alternatives and rationale are recorded.
* Consequences and follow-up actions are identified.
* Status is tracked for future review.
