# Test Strategy

## Purpose

Document the overall approach to verification and validation, ensuring testing is purposeful and aligned with goals.

## When Used

Use this template once requirements and design are sufficiently understood and the team needs a shared testing plan.

## Produced By

Forge-Test-Strategy or equivalent test strategy activity.

## Consumed By

* QA teams
* Engineers
* Product owners
* Reviewers

## Template

### Objectives

Describe the testing goals and what the strategy intends to achieve.

### Scope

Define what is included and excluded from the testing effort.

### Quality Risks

List the top risks and areas of uncertainty that testing must address.

### Test Levels

Describe the levels of testing that will be used, such as:

* Unit testing
* Integration testing
* End-to-end testing
* Regression testing
* Performance testing
* Security testing

### Verification Approach

Explain how the work will be verified, including test types, coverage expectations, and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities

Identify who owns the testing activities and who is accountable for quality outcomes.

### Entry and Exit Criteria

Describe the conditions required to begin and complete the testing effort.

## Completion Criteria

* Testing objectives are clearly defined.
* Scope and risk areas are documented.
* The verification approach is specified.
* Responsibilities are assigned.
* Entry and exit criteria are defined.
