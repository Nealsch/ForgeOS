# Requirements Discovery

## Purpose

Capture stakeholder needs, business goals, user context, and high-level requirements before the solution is defined.

## When Used

Use this template during early requirements discovery, problem definition, or stakeholder investigation.

## Produced By

Forge-Requirements-Elicitation or equivalent discovery activity.

## Consumed By

* Forge-Requirements-Analysis
* Forge-Grill
* Forge-User-Story-Definition
* Architects and engineers preparing solution options

## Template

### Problem Statement

Describe the problem or opportunity the project is intended to address.

### Stakeholders

List the stakeholders, their roles, and what they care about.

### Users

Describe the intended users or user groups and their needs.

### Objectives

List the business or user objectives the solution must achieve.

### Functional Requirements

Describe the required capabilities at a high level.

### Non-Functional Requirements

Capture quality attributes such as performance, security, reliability, usability, and compliance.

### Constraints

List any known constraints, dependencies, or assumptions.

### Open Questions

Capture unresolved questions and areas that require clarification.

## Completion Criteria

* Stakeholder needs are clearly documented.
* Objectives are aligned with business or user value.
* Functional and non-functional expectations are captured.
* Known constraints and assumptions are identified.
* Open questions are listed for follow-up.
