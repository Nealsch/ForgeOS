# Acceptance Criteria

## Purpose

Define clear, verifiable criteria that describe the expected behaviour and success conditions for a requirement or user story.

## When Used

Use this template once a user story or requirement is sufficiently understood and ready for implementation and testing.

## Produced By

Forge-Acceptance-Criteria or equivalent acceptance criteria definition activity.

## Consumed By

* Engineers
* QA
* Product owners
* Reviewers

## Template

### Acceptance Criteria

List the observable conditions that must be met for the work to be considered complete.

### Success Conditions

Describe the positive outcomes that indicate the requirement is satisfied.

### Failure Conditions

Describe the conditions under which the work is not acceptable.

### Business Rules

List any rules or constraints that affect verification.

### Validation Notes

Capture any special validation requirements or test considerations.

### Related Requirements

Link the acceptance criteria to the relevant user story, requirement, or decision.

## Completion Criteria

* The acceptance criteria are clear and testable.
* Success and failure conditions are explicitly stated.
* Related requirements and dependencies are identified.
* The criteria are suitable for implementation and verification.
