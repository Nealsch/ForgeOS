# Requirements Specification

## Purpose

Define the analysed requirements with clarity, traceability, and acceptance context.

## When Used

Use this template after requirements discovery to create a stable requirements baseline for design and planning.

## Produced By

Forge-Requirements-Analysis or equivalent requirements analysis activity.

## Consumed By

* Architects
* Engineers
* QA
* Product owners

## Template

### Overview

Summarise the business problem, scope, and key goals.

### Requirement Categories

Group requirements into categories such as:

* Functional Requirements
* Non-Functional Requirements
* Security Requirements
* Compliance Requirements
* Performance Requirements

### Requirement Details

For each requirement, include:

* ID
* Title
* Description
* Priority
* Acceptance Criteria
* Related User Stories
* Related Decisions
* Dependencies
* Assumptions

### Constraints

Document constraints that impact the solution.

### Risks and Issues

Capture risks, issues, and open questions related to requirements.

## Completion Criteria

* Requirements are clearly expressed and structured.
* Each requirement has sufficient context for design and implementation.
* Dependencies, assumptions, and risks are recorded.
* Traceability links to user stories, decisions, and acceptance criteria are identified.
