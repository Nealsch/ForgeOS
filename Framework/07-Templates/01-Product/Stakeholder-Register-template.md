# Stakeholder Register

## Purpose

Record stakeholder details, expectations, and influence so that stakeholder needs are understood and managed.

## When Used

Use this template during requirements discovery and stakeholder interviews.

## Produced By

Forge-Requirements-Elicitation or equivalent stakeholder analysis activity.

## Consumed By

* Product owners
* Architects
* Engineers
* Project leads

## Template

### Stakeholder Name

### Role

### Interests and Goals

### Influence and Impact

### Engagement Approach

### Communication Preferences

### Concerns and Risks

## Completion Criteria

* Stakeholders are identified and described.
* Roles, interests, and influence are recorded.
* Communication and engagement preferences are noted.
* Risks or concerns are captured.
