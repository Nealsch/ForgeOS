# Roadmap

## Purpose

Document planned work and timing to align stakeholders and guide delivery decisions.

## When Used

Use this template during release planning, prioritisation, or roadmap review.

## Produced By

Forge-Prioritisation or equivalent product planning activity.

## Consumed By

* Product owners
* Delivery planners
* Engineering teams
* Stakeholders

## Template

### Strategic Themes

List the high-level themes or goals the roadmap supports.

### Planned Releases or Increments

For each planned release or increment, capture:

* Timebox or target date
* Key outcomes
* Primary work items
* Dependencies

### Prioritised Work

List the highest-priority work items, grouped by release or theme.

### Risks and Assumptions

Capture risks, constraints, and assumptions that affect the roadmap.

### Status or Confidence

Describe the current confidence level and any factors that may change the plan.

## Completion Criteria

* Planned work is mapped to releases or themes.
* Timing and priorities are documented.
* Dependencies and assumptions are recorded.
* The roadmap is usable for planning and stakeholder alignment.
