# Compliance Summary

## Purpose

Document the outcome of a compliance review, including what was assessed, any gaps found, and next steps.

## When Used

Use this template after a standards or compliance review of engineering outputs, processes, or documentation.

## Produced By

Forge-Standards-Compliance or equivalent compliance review activity.

## Consumed By

* Security reviewers
* Compliance stakeholders
* Engineers
* Project leads

## Template

### Work Reviewed

Describe the scope of the review and the artefacts or activities assessed.

### SDLC Phase

Note the current lifecycle phase during the review.

### Applicable Standards

List the standards, policies, or controls that were evaluated.

### Compliance Status

Summarise overall compliance and highlight any gaps.

### Validated Areas

Document the specific areas that were reviewed and found compliant.

### Issues Identified

List non-compliance findings, risks, or concerns.

### Required Actions

Describe the corrective actions or remediation needed.

### Approval Recommendation

Provide a recommendation for whether the work can proceed, needs revision, or requires additional review.

## Completion Criteria

* The review scope is clearly documented.
* Applicable standards are identified.
* Compliance status and findings are recorded.
* Required actions are listed.
* A recommendation is provided for next steps.
