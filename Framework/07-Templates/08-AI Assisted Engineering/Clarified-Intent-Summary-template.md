# Clarified Intent Summary

## Purpose

Document the clarified objective, problem statement, and next steps after a clarification or intent refinement activity.

## When Used

Use this template after a Forge-Grill session or similar clarification process when task intent must be captured before proceeding.

## Produced By

Forge-Grill or equivalent clarification activity.

## Consumed By

* Product owners
* Architects
* Engineers
* QA
* Delivery planners

## Template

### Objective

Summarise the clarified goal or desired outcome.

### Problem Statement

Describe the problem or need being addressed.

### Scope

Define what is included and excluded.

### Key Requirements

List the key requirements or acceptance conditions that were clarified.

### Constraints and Assumptions

Capture any constraints, dependencies, or assumptions identified.

### Next Steps

Document the recommended next actions or follow-up activities.

### Open Questions

List unresolved questions or areas needing further clarification.

## Completion Criteria

* The objective is clearly captured.
* The problem statement aligns with stakeholder intent.
* Scope, requirements, and constraints are documented.
* Next steps are identified.
* Open questions are listed for follow-up.
