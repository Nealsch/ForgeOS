# Session Summary

## Purpose

Document the results of an engineering session, including completed work, decisions made, and recommended next steps.

## When Used

Use this template at the end of a session or whenever handoff information needs to be captured for the next work period.

## Produced By

Forge-Session-Management or equivalent session documentation activity.

## Consumed By

* Engineers
* Product owners
* QA
* Reviewers
* Next session participants

## Template

### Session Objective

Summarise the objective of the session.

### Work Completed

Describe what was completed during the session.

### Decisions Made

Capture decisions, rationale, and any changes to scope or direction.

### Issues Encountered

Document any problems, blockers, or risks discovered.

### Files Changed

List the key files or artefacts updated.

### Next Actions

Document the recommended follow-up work.

### Outstanding Questions

List any unresolved questions or clarifications needed.

## Completion Criteria

* The session objective is recorded.
* Completed work and decisions are documented.
* Issues and blockers are described.
* Next actions are identified.
* Outstanding questions are captured.
