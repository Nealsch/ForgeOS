# Architecture Decision Record

## Purpose

Document an architectural decision, its context, the alternatives considered, and the rationale behind the chosen approach.

## When Used

Use this template when an architecture decision affects system structure, performance, security, maintainability, or implementation.

## Produced By

Forge-Architecture-Decision-Records or equivalent decision documentation activity.

## Consumed By

* Architects
* Engineers
* Reviewers
* Future maintainers

## Template

### Decision Title

Summarise the decision being made.

### Context

Describe the situation, goals, and constraints that led to the decision.

### Decision

State the chosen option explicitly.

### Alternatives Considered

List the reasonable alternatives and why they were not selected.

### Rationale

Explain why the chosen decision is the best fit for the context.

### Consequences

Document the expected outcomes, impacts, and trade-offs of the decision.

### Related Requirements and Decisions

Link to relevant requirements, user stories, or prior decisions.

## Completion Criteria

* The decision is clearly stated.
* Context and rationale are documented.
* Alternatives and trade-offs are recorded.
* Expected consequences and dependencies are identified.
