# Security Risk Assessment

## Purpose

Document security risks, their severity, and the actions required to mitigate or accept them.

## When Used

Use this template after threat modelling or security analysis and before implementation or compliance review.

## Produced By

Forge-Threat-Modelling or equivalent security risk assessment activity.

## Consumed By

* Architects
* Engineers
* Security reviewers
* Compliance stakeholders

## Template

### Risk Summary

Describe the security risk and the system area it affects.

### Impact

Explain the potential impact if the risk materialises.

### Likelihood

Estimate the likelihood of the risk occurring.

### Risk Rating

Record the risk level using the chosen scale (e.g. high, medium, low).

### Mitigation Actions

Document the controls, design changes, or process steps to reduce the risk.

### Residual Risk

Capture the risk that remains after mitigation.

### Responsible Owner

Identify who is responsible for managing the risk.

### Review Notes

Add any additional observations, assumptions, or follow-up requirements.

## Completion Criteria

* Security risks are defined clearly.
* Impact and likelihood are documented.
* Mitigation actions and residual risk are recorded.
* Ownership is assigned.
* The assessment is ready for review and tracking.
