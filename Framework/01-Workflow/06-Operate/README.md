# Operate

## Purpose

The Operate phase ensures that the released software continues to deliver value by maintaining its availability, reliability, security and performance in its operational environment.

Its purpose is to monitor, support and maintain the production system while responding to incidents, managing operational risks and identifying opportunities for continuous improvement.

Operation is an ongoing activity that continues until the product is retired.

---

# Objectives

The objectives of the Operate phase are to:

* Maintain system availability.
* Monitor system health and performance.
* Support users.
* Resolve operational incidents.
* Manage operational risks.
* Maintain security.
* Perform routine maintenance.
* Collect operational metrics.
* Capture improvement opportunities.
* Ensure operational stability.

# Deliverables

The Operate phase should produce:

* Operational Metrics
* Incident Reports
* Root Cause Analyses
* Performance Reports
* Updated Operational Documentation
* Updated Project Journal
* Improvement Candidates
* Maintenance Records
* Operational Risk Updates

These deliverables become valuable inputs to the Improve phase.

---

# Entry Criteria

The Operate phase may begin when:

* The Release phase has successfully passed its quality gate.
* The production deployment has been verified.
* Operational ownership has been established.
* Monitoring has been enabled.
* Support procedures are available.

---

# Exit Criteria

The Operate phase is continuous and normally concludes only when:

* The product reaches end-of-life.
* Responsibility is transferred to another operational team.
* The product is formally retired.

Otherwise, the Operate phase continually feeds improvements back into the SDLC.

---

# Quality Gate

Operational health is considered acceptable when:

* System availability meets agreed objectives.
* Critical incidents are being managed appropriately.
* Security monitoring is active.
* Performance remains within acceptable limits.
* Operational documentation remains current.
* Improvement opportunities are being captured.
* The DevOps Engineer confirms the system remains stable and supportable.

---

# Next Phase

Operational experience continuously informs the **Improve** phase.

Defects, enhancement requests, technical debt and operational lessons identified during operation should be prioritised and fed back into the product backlog for future planning.

---

# ForgeOS Principle

The Operate phase answers the question:

> **"Is the software continuing to deliver value reliably and safely in production?"**

Successful operation is measured not only by uptime, but by the ability to support users, maintain system health, respond effectively to change and continuously generate insights that improve future releases.
